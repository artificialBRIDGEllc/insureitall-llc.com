**Client:** [LEGAL NAME]  
**Effective:** [DATE]

1. **Status.** Independent contractor. No employment, no benefits, no withheld taxes.
2. **Work.** Described in SOW #[ ].
3. **Fee.** $[AMOUNT], due Net [15]. Late: 1.5%/month or legal max.
4. **IP default.** Client owns what Client supplies. Contractor owns tools, methods, characters, engines, and leftovers. Client gets a **license to use the deliverable on Client’s domain**, not an assignment, unless a signed writing says “assignment” and names the asset.
5. **Confidentiality.** 3 years. No SSN / MBI collection unless a BAA and a separate PHI SOW exist.
6. **Liability cap.** Fees paid under the SOW that caused the claim. No lost-profits damages.
7. **Term.** Work complete on acceptance (7-day defect window). Convenience kill: bill time + [kill fee or 0].
8. **Law.** [STATE]. Entire agreement. Writing required to change it.
